## 🧩 About

**7 Days to Die Trainer** is a comprehensive mod menu for 7 Days to Die on Windows featuring god mode, unlimited stamina, no hunger and thirst, instant craft, item spawner, teleport, and zombie control.

Based on popular mods like **7D2D Mod Launcher**, **Undead Legacy Helper**, and **Bdubyahs Mods**.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎮 Core Hacks
- God Mode — become invulnerable to zombies, fall damage, and traps
- Unlimited Stamina — sprint, swing, and mine without fatigue
- No Hunger & No Thirst — survival meters stay full forever
- No Infection — immune to zombie virus and infections
- Instant Craft — craft and repair without delays or workbenches

### 🎨 Player & World
- Item Spawner — spawn weapons, ammo, food, and building materials
- Teleport — jump to waypoints, traders, and POIs
- Experience Multiplier — boost XP gain for fast leveling
- Skill Point Unlocker — unlock perk points on demand

### 🏋️ Training & Utility
- Zombie Freeze — pause zombie AI for safe exploration
- Instant Loot Respawn — refresh containers and POIs
- Time Control — set day and night cycle length
- Unlimited Ammo — no reload or ammo consumption
